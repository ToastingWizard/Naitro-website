Why NaiTRO

Your day is full of tiny resets.

Open the same apps. Find the right window. Search for the tab you already had open. Put everything back where you need it. Those little steps add up and pull you away from the work itself.

NaiTRO gives your Windows desktop a simple voice-and-text control layer. It works with the apps, folders, websites, and browser you already use, so a short request can get you back into your task.

Works across your desktop

Launch apps and folders, bring windows forward, arrange them, and find supported browser tabs from one place.

Fits the way you already work

Use voice when your hands are busy, text when they are not, and follow up naturally.

Makes repeatable setups one command

Build custom modes from the apps, websites, and folders you open together every day.
